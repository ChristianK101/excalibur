      How this lines up with Clover. Gross sales and discounts come off the ticket lines;
      tax, tips, refunds and what was collected come off the payments, which is where Clover records them.
      Net sales is gross less discounts, before tax — the same figure Clover leads its sales report with.
      Days are Pacific, so a report matches the lounge’s day rather than UTC.
      


      Total collected is payments plus tips less refunds. It will not equal net sales,
      because it includes tax and tips and excludes anything sent back.
      


      Pull New Sales fetches whatever changed since the last pull. Pulling twice cannot
      double-count: everything is stored under Clover’s own ids, so a second pull overwrites rather than
      adds. A run stops after 800 orders and says there is more — press it again to carry on.
      


      This is a trading report, not a tax return. It shows what the register did. Anything
      that happens outside Clover — cash paid out, house comps rung as something else, an order voided
      before it was paid — is not here.
      


      Only owner accounts can open this page. Managers reach the Team and Payroll pages;
      neither shows sales.
