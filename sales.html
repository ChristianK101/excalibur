      Where these numbers come from. Every order and line item on the register is copied
      into the site’s own database, so a report never waits on Clover and never changes anything there.
      Days are Pacific, matching the lounge’s day rather than UTC.
      


      Pull New Sales fetches whatever has changed since the last pull — run it whenever
      you want fresh figures. The backfill buttons are for the first run, or after a long gap. Pulling twice
      cannot double-count: orders are stored under Clover’s own ids, so a second pull overwrites rather
      than adds.
      


      Refunded and exchanged lines are left out of the item counts. Gross sales is the sum of
      order totals before any tax or tip breakdown, so treat it as a trading figure, not an accounting one.
      


      “Sold Nothing” lists items on the Clover menu that rang up zero in the range.
      Hidden items are skipped. If a bottle is missing from that list and from the table above, it is not on
      the register’s menu at all.
