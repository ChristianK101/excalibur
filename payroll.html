How these are worked out. Hours come from completed clock entries, in Pacific time. California overtime is applied per workweek (Sunday to Saturday): over 8 hours in a day at 1.5×, over 12 at 2×, over 40 straight-time hours in a week at 1.5×, and a seventh consecutive workday at 1.5× for the first 8 hours then 2×.

Tips are what a manager entered for days in this range. They are paid on top and are never multiplied by an overtime rate, because they are not wages.

This is a gross figure. No tax, withholding, or deductions are calculated here — that is for your payroll provider. Meal and rest break premiums are not included either.
