Scheduling, in three ways

Most weeks you will only need the first one. Times are always the lounge’s local time.

1
Copy Last Week — the quick way

If this week looks like last week, set Week beginning to the week you are filling, then press Copy Last Week. Everyone who was scheduled the week before gets the same days and the same times again. Then just fix whatever is different — remove a shift, or add one.

Pressing it twice is safe. Anything already scheduled is left alone rather than being doubled up.

2
Add Shifts — for a standing rota

Pick the person, set their start and end time, then tap the days they work — Mon Tue Wed and so on. Choose the week it starts, pick how long it repeats, and press Add Shifts.

Example: Anna, 4:00 PM to 11:00 PM, tap Thu Fri Sat, repeat for 8 weeks. That is 24 shifts from one press.

3
+1 day / +1 week — for one-offs

Every shift in the list below has these two buttons. They copy that single shift to the next day, or to the same day next week. Useful when someone picks up one extra shift.

★
Worth knowing

A shift that ends earlier than it starts is treated as running past midnight — so 4:00 PM to 1:00 AM is a nine hour shift ending the next morning, which is what you want for a close.

Scheduling someone does not clock them in. Staff still tap Clock In on their own Schedules page when they arrive. The schedule is what they are meant to work; the clock is what they actually worked.

Everything you do here is recorded with your name in the owner’s activity log.

You can reopen this any time with the ? button next to Schedule Shifts.
